

# 🎮 Dodge the Spikes!

Welcome to our little game - your mission is simple:  
Survive as long as you can without getting hit by the spikes!

---

## 🕹️ How to Play
- Move left/right with the Arrow Keys or A/D.  
- Jump with the **Space** key.  
- Hold **Shift** to sprint and move faster.  
- Beware - the spikes bounce all around and never give up!

---

## 📜 Rules
1. The player must avoid the spikes.  
2. If a spike touches the player → **Game Over**.  
3. Your score is the **time you survive** (in seconds).  
4. The longer you survive - the higher your score!  

---

## 🏆 Winning
There’s no true “end” - the goal is to last as long as possible and beat your high score.  

---

## 🔄 Options
- At the end, a Game Over screen will show your score.  
- The **Restart** button lets you quickly try again.  

---

## 😎 Pro Tip
Spikes bounce unpredictably, so don’t stand still - keep moving!
